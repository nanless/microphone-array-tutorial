LOCATA 实录试听对照 / LOCATA recording, before and after

Dataset: Christine Evers, Heinrich W. Löllmann, Heinrich Mellmann, Alexander Schmidt,
Hendrik Barfuss, Patrick A. Naylor, and Walter Kellermann. Data Corpus for the
IEEE-AASP Challenge on Acoustic Source Localization and Tracking (LOCATA), v1.
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/3630471
Dataset license: Open Data Commons Attribution 1.0
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/
The original speech played in Tasks 1–2 is CSTR VCTK speech, likewise credited
and identified as Open Data Commons Attribution in LOCATA documentation §3.1.
https://www.locata.lms.tf.fau.de/files/2020/01/Documentation_LOCATA_final_release_V1.pdf
Challenge paper: https://doi.org/10.1109/TASLP.2020.2990485

Source take: dev.zip, dev/task2/recording1/dicit/audio_array_dicit.wav.
A denotes loudspeaker 3; B denotes loudspeaker 4. Both clips cover the entire
6.302667-second take at 48 kHz. Before is channel 7. After is offline delay and
sum over all 15 channels, steered with the optical position of A and measured
microphone positions. Both clips receive the same fixed gain of four and are
converted to mono PCM16 without separate peak normalization. The displayed
power changes use the release's A-only and B-only activity labels, including
room reverberation and background. These numbers do not quantify source power
during overlapping speech.

Localization selects frames whose centers have an A-only activity label, uses
375–3000 Hz, and calls the repository
SRP-PHAT implementation. Scan angles are relative to global positive y, with
positive x to the right. The optical measurement supplies the position markers
and the comparison direction. The scan estimates a bearing, not a range.

MANIFEST.json records the upstream members and checksums, configuration,
angle scan, source geometry and integer PCM energies. With the named members
extracted into PATH/dev and FFmpeg on PATH, run from the repository root:
  .venv/bin/python -m scripts.course_home.prepare_locata PATH --check
Omit --check only when intentionally regenerating the pair and its manifest.
